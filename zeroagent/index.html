Free · Local · No account required

Your coding agents, running on your own Mac.

ZeroAgent dispatches a team of coding agents, one per ticket, each in its own git worktree. Every worker shows up as a creature on a canvas you can watch, drag, and talk to, from the first commit to the landed PR. It runs entirely on your machine, free, with nothing to sign up for.

The canvas is the product

Workers are not rows in a table. They are creatures on a 2D canvas: status reads at a glance from motion and colour, so you can watch several agents at once without losing track of any of them, and a click opens the transcript.

Free and local

Everything below costs nothing to run and needs no account. This is the whole app, not a trial.

Lead agent and worker dispatch

Hand it a ticket and it spins up a worker in its own worktree. Message it, watch it, or retire it, all from one conversation.

A worktree per worker

Branch management, retire and sweep, all the way to a landed PR, with no parent link holding a worker hostage to the session that spawned it.

Bring your own agent and keys

ZeroAgent drives the coding agent CLI already on your Mac, on your own model keys. Nothing routes through a server of ours.

Free today, a relay later
